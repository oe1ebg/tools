# v0.6.0

This release gives **Netlog** a memory across nets: each station's history
from earlier sessions, open follow-ups you can close or carry into today's
net, and search by series and station. Series can be folded, summarised and
backed up on their own, and My Turn shows the current and previous round
together. All tools are easier to read and use with a keyboard, screen reader
or phone after an accessibility review, and a rare stalled update in Chrome
is fixed. Fictional example backups let you try Netlog, Confirm and Notfunk
with realistic data.

## Netlog

- **Station history:** a "Verlauf" / "History" panel under the station in
  Live Notes lists the station's earlier sessions on this device, newest
  first, across series and ad-hoc nets: date, net, the name and call as
  recorded then, and the notes with round, topic and reply state. Every entry
  opens the exact note in its session; `b` / Back returns with your draft
  kept.
- **Matching stations:** the same station profile or the same full callsign
  (`OE0QXA` = `OE0QXA/P`) counts automatically. Partial calls such as
  `OE0QX?` or a matching name only appear as "possibly the same station".
  You decide "Same station" or "Not the same"; both can be undone, and the
  recorded entries are never rewritten.
- **Open follow-ups from earlier sessions:** open on-air replies and
  after-net tasks come first in the history. Mark one **done** where it is,
  or carry it forward as a task or into **My Turn**. A carried item shows
  where it came from ("↪ from 2026-10-02 · Wiener Runde #1 · OE0QXA") and
  links back; the original is closed. Nothing from an earlier session enters
  My Turn without that click. One undo takes both steps back.
- **Search (`/`):** filter by series, or sessions without a series; search
  only the selected station, across all its sessions; round topics are
  searchable and open in Net Control.
- **Series:** series cards fold open and closed, and the device remembers
  which are open. Each card has a summary, for example "4 sessions ·
  2026-06-03 – 2026-06-24 · ⌀ 7 stations · 12 different stations · 89 notes ·
  8 open".
- **Back up and restore one series:** "Back up" on a series card downloads
  that series with all its sessions in one JSON file. "Restore…" on the same
  card accepts only a backup of that series; it checks the file first and
  skips sessions that are already there.
- **My Turn with both rounds:** the round notes now show the current and the
  previous round together, grouped by station, each note tagged with its
  round (R4, R3). The Current/Previous switch and the `p` key are gone.
- **Printed report:** notes and replies appear only when "with notes and
  replies" is ticked; it is off by default. The export hint says which files
  contain notes.
- **Layout:** the station heading in Live Notes takes two lines instead of
  five. Click a session's date and title to open it; ↑/↓ for the speaking
  order sit right under the station list. On phones, history entries use the
  full width.

## All tools: accessibility

After a review of all tools (WCAG 2.2 AA, automated checks with axe-core in
light, dark and phone views), the automated checks report no violations in
any view.

- **Contrast:** the blue of buttons and active toggles is slightly darker,
  and muted text in dark mode, warnings and the ADIF hint are easier to read.
  App icons keep their colour.
- **Screen readers:** suggestion lists (callsigns, places) are announced as
  lists with options; button names start with their visible text, for
  example Confirm's "Bearb.".
- **Targets and small screens:** header toggles, help and back links, ADIF's
  "×" buttons and the ADIF/SOTA calendar buttons are at least 24×24 px.
  Every view fits a 320 px wide screen without scrolling sideways, including
  ADIF with a loaded log and Confirm's storage notice.

## All offline tools: updates

- An update could stall in Chrome and Edge: after "Update" or "Erneut
  versuchen" the new version sometimes did not take over, and the page just
  stayed. Your input was saved, but the update only happened on a later
  reload. The page now reloads by itself after a few seconds and loads the
  new version.

## Example data

- [`examples/backups/`](https://github.com/oe1ebg/tools/tree/v0.6.0/examples/backups)
  contains fictional backups to import into Netlog (3 series, 12 sessions),
  Confirm (4 logs) and Notfunk (a logistics exercise with 30 messages), each
  also as a small example. The README there explains what to try. They are
  in the repository only, not in the release bundle.

## Limitations

- Netlog's history covers sessions on this device only. Nothing is looked up
  or shared; to see another device's sessions, restore its backup.
- Netlog's help is in the app only; there is no separate manual yet
  ([#88](https://github.com/oe1ebg/tools/issues/88)).
- Open from the accessibility review
  ([#108](https://github.com/oe1ebg/tools/issues/108)): tables on phones in
  Confirm and Notfunk, Confirm's header on phones, the ADIF column order, and
  tests at 200 % zoom, with forced colours and with a screen reader.

## Before updating

- Export important logs and verify that the backup files arrived before
  updating, as for every release.
- For USB or `file://` use, replace downloaded single-file copies with this
  release's copies.
- A Netlog series backup can be restored by v0.5.0 too, as an ordinary
  backup.
- Server operators: the header configuration is unchanged since v0.5.0.
