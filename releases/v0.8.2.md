# v0.8.2

A documentation release: the **Notfunk manual** is reorganised into
`tutorial`, `manual` and `reference`, and **every page now has an English
edition** next to the German one. The tools themselves behave exactly as in
v0.8.1.

## Notfunk-Meldebuch manual

- **Complete English edition:** the introduction, the tutorial, the reference
  of the form, logbook, printout, backup, notices and keyboard, and the
  sources are available in English. The tool itself stays German, so the
  English pages quote its buttons and messages in German with a translation.
- **New structure:** the pages are grouped in the directories `tutorial/`,
  `manual/` and `reference/`, and the start page lists them in these three
  groups. The Sources moved from the start page to their own reference page.
- **File names:** all pages have English names and a language suffix, for
  example `manual/message.de.md` and `manual/message.en.md`, so the two
  editions sit next to each other and a site generator with language suffixes
  (such as Zensical) can pick them up.

## Before updating

- Nothing to do for users: stored data, backups and exports are unchanged,
  and the tools' behaviour is unchanged.
- Sites that render `docs/` in their own theme (such as oe1ebg.at): the
  Notfunk manual pages moved again. They are now
  `docs/notfunk-anleitung/{tutorial,manual,reference}/<name>.de.md` and
  `<name>.en.md`, with the start pages `index.de.md` and `index.en.md`.
  Pictures are linked as `../../img/…` from the sub pages.
- The address `/tools/notfunk-anleitung/` (German start page) stays, so the
  links in the tool still work. The bundled pages below it have new addresses,
  for example `/tools/notfunk-anleitung/manual/message/`; the English edition
  of a page is the address with `.en` appended to its name, for example
  `/tools/notfunk-anleitung/manual/message.en/`.

## Limitations

- The message format of the Notfunk-Meldebuch is still a draft and not agreed
  with the Notfunkreferat Wien and the Meldesammelstelle.
- The English pages reuse the screenshots of the German interface, and the
  diagram labels of the introduction are German in both editions.
- The new English pages were not reviewed by a native speaker.
- The limitations of v0.8.1 about screen readers, forced colours, the dark
  theme and unopened sources still apply.
- The tool still asks for MESZ or MEZ when a time falls into the repeated hour
  of the autumn clock change;
  [#153](https://github.com/oe1ebg/tools/issues/153) proposes to remove the
  choice.
