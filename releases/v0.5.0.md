# v0.5.0

This release adds **Netlog**, a new tool for amateur radio nets: notes per
station, on-air reminders for your own turn, net control, rounds, search and
backups. It runs offline in German and English, like the other tools. The
local time choice in Confirm and SOTA Alerts now names your time zone, and a
failed update check stays visible in every offline tool.

## Netlog (new)

- **Sessions and series:** start a net right away (ad hoc), or as a numbered
  session of a series with its usual repeater and net control. An ad-hoc
  session can be put into a series later. Join a net that is already running
  in any round.
- **Stations:** check in with callsign and name. Partial callsigns such as
  `OE0QX?` are fine and can be resolved later. Name and QTH come from the
  offline Austrian callsign list. Status: active, paused, monitoring ("im
  Hintergrund"), unconfirmed, checked out. Checked-out stations are struck
  through and skipped by `j`/`k`.
- **Live notes:** short notes per station and round. Enter saves at once.
  Unfinished text survives reloads, updates and language switches.
- **My Turn:** what you wanted to answer or mention, open replies from earlier
  rounds first, then the notes of the current or previous round. Your own
  station opens My Turn instead of notes about yourself.
- **Speaking order:** central control / Sternverkehr (every station gives back
  to the net control, which calls the next), round-robin / Reihum (stations
  pass on in turn) and free-form / Freie Runde (spontaneous, no fixed order,
  no net control needed), shown as a small picture and in the station list.
  The panel above the list shows who is speaking now and who is next; a
  banner says when it is your turn. **▶ Next** or `.` moves the net on;
  "Start round N+1" appears when everyone has spoken. Selecting a station
  with `j`/`k` or a click only opens it; it never records a turn.
- **Net Control:** order, status, net control handovers with corrected
  times, removed stations, rounds and their topics.
- **Continuous log, search, undo:** every note in time order. Search across
  all sessions on the device. Undo and redo for every change, kept across
  reloads.
- **Export:** stations and notes as CSV, check-ins as ADIF 3.1.7 (one QSO per
  station, with the session's repeater, frequency and mode), and a printable
  session report. The ADIF export lists anything it leaves out before you
  download.
- **Repeater, frequency, mode:** separate fields for a series and a session.
  A direct net needs no repeater; the frequency can also be just a band, such
  as "2m" for a 2m SSB net.
- **Backup:** one JSON file for everything or one session. Restoring checks
  the file first and changes nothing if it is invalid.
- **Offline:** works offline once loaded from the server. There is also a
  single file, `netlog-offline.html` (about 1 MB), for a USB stick or a laptop
  without a server.
- **Time:** times in UTC or local time; the switch next to the clock names
  your time zone, e.g. "Europe/Vienna (UTC+2)".

## Confirmation log and SOTA Alerts Map

- The local time choice names your time zone and its offset, for example
  "Europe/Vienna (UTC+2)", instead of only "local".

## All offline tools

- A failed check for updates stays visible. Before, a slower earlier check
  could clear the "Update-Prüfung fehlgeschlagen" note again.

## Limitations

- Netlog is for one person on one device. Sharing a net between stations,
  remote check-ins and online lookups are planned separately (see
  [#68](https://github.com/oe1ebg/tools/issues/68) for the follow-up issues).
- Netlog's help is in the app only; there is no separate manual yet
  ([#88](https://github.com/oe1ebg/tools/issues/88)).
- The browser keeps the Netlog offline file's data separately from the
  installed Netlog. Use a backup to move sessions between the two.
- Netlog's ADIF export contains check-ins only, never notes. Logbook programs
  need a mode: set it under "This session" in Net Control.

## Before updating

- Export important logs and verify that the backup files arrived before
  updating, as for every release.
- For USB or `file://` use, replace downloaded single-file copies with this
  release's copies. Netlog's offline file is new.
- Server operators: the supplied header configuration has changed. It adds
  Netlog's inline load-failure script (by hash) and the inline policy for
  `netlog-offline.html`. Follow the
  [deployment instructions](https://github.com/oe1ebg/tools/blob/v0.5.0/README.md#updating-a-deployment-atomically)
  and update your copy of `deploy/nginx.conf.example` or
  `deploy/htaccess.example`.
