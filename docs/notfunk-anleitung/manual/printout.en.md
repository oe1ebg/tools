# Printout and PDF

[← Notfunk logbook (English)](../index.en.md) · [Open the Notfunk logbook](../../notfunk/index.html)
{ .doc-nav }

The logbook produces **PDF files**: a single message as a form, the empty
form, and the whole logbook. Printing is done in the print dialog of the PDF
viewer (scale "actual size" or 100 %).

## The empty form for paper

The logbook also runs without internet. Even so, be prepared for **paper-only**
operation in case the device or the power fails. **So print the forms before
the operation**: put a stack next to pen and radio. If the device fails, carry
on on paper and enter the messages into the logbook later ("Nachtrag vom
Papier"); write the number from the paper into the notes so that both match.

The message form to print (A4 portrait, one page). It has the same fields as
the form in the logbook; choices such as urgency, transmission and type of
message are ticked or circled.

[Download the empty form (PDF)](../../downloads/notfunk-vordruck.pdf){ download="notfunk-vordruck.pdf" } (German)

In the logbook you also find it on the start page under "Leeres Formular
(PDF)" and under "Drucken / PDF…".

Filling it in on paper: write the wording in the large field, have it **read
back** if possible (not mandatory) and tick the box, and enter the handover or
transmission at the bottom. Leave the block "Nur von der Meldesammelstelle
auszufüllen" (to be filled in by the Meldesammelstelle only) empty.

## A message as a form

**"Ausdruck / PDF"** in the message (or "PDF" in the logbook row) writes the
message into the form and opens the PDF in a new tab; if the browser blocks
the tab, it is downloaded.

![Printout of a message](../../img/notfunk-ausdruck.png)

The printout shows all details of the message and the operation, the
direction (the chosen one ticked), date and time in Vienna local time, and at
the bottom the handover or transmission. A very long message continues on
following pages; nothing is cut off.

## The logbook as a PDF

**"Drucken / PDF…"** in the logbook, optionally with a period (from/to with
date and time; a date alone = the whole day), then **"Meldebuch als PDF"**.
The PDF (A4 landscape) has the logbook's columns, the full content of every
message, and on every page the operation, the period and "Seite n von N" (page
n of N). Emergency and urgent are recognisable even when printed in black and
white.
